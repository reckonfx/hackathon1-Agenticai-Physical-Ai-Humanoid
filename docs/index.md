**Written by Aamir Ahmed Shamsi with Claude AI**

Welcome to the comprehensive interactive textbook on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics! This textbook combines cutting-edge educational content with advanced AI capabilities to provide an unparalleled learning experience.

This interactive textbook covers 7 comprehensive modules on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics:

- **Module 1**: Introduction & Physical AI Foundations
- **Module 2**: ROS 2 Fundamentals
- **Module 3**: Robot Simulation
- **Module 4**: NVIDIA Isaac Platform
- **Module 5**: Humanoid Robot Development
- **Module 6**: VLA Robotics
- **Module 7**: Capstone Project

## Interactive Learning

This textbook features a state-of-the-art RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chatbot that can answer questions about any topic covered in the course. The chatbot has access to all textbook content and can provide detailed explanations, examples, and references to specific sections.

### Key Features

- **Intelligent Q&A**: Ask complex questions about Physical AI and robotics concepts
- **Contextual Understanding**: The chatbot understands the context of your questions
- **Source Attribution**: All answers include references to specific textbook sections
- **Real-time Responses**: Get immediate answers to enhance your learning

## How to Use

1. Navigate through the textbook modules using the sidebar
2. Read the content and explore the concepts
3. Use the chatbot widget (bottom-right corner) to ask questions
4. Get personalized explanations and clarifications

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this textbook, you will:

- Understand the fundamentals of Physical AI and embodied intelligence
- Master ROS 2 concepts and practical applications
- Learn robot simulation techniques and tools
- Explore NVIDIA Isaac platform capabilities
- Develop skills in humanoid robot control and development
- Understand Vision-Language-Action (VLA) robotics systems
- Complete a comprehensive capstone project integrating all concepts
